=== Readout from trust task force: Scott

🎥 _23'11":_ Scott described three work streams. The task force has paused its work on a trust-registry query protocol reference in the identity assertion while it prioritizes a CAWG certificate policy for X.509 certificates. The draft covers individual, organizational, and sponsor-validated certificates; the task force will revisit whether to retain S/MIME use and continue refining the policy.

The task force is also developing a framework template for industry governance frameworks and conformance programs. It plans to draw on the Trust Over IP governance toolkit and other existing deliverables. Scott encouraged members to review and participate in the work.

The certificate-policy draft is now in AsciiDoc and available for review. The group discussed using GitHub for comments; Eric has a PR to move the document there and carried over editorial comments as TODO boxes. Scott asked members to review the draft before the next Trust Task Force meeting.

=== Review/triage new issues

🎥 _40'29":_ Matthew said conformance testing exposed ambiguity in the identity assertion's status-code URL: the specification should require a JUMBF URI reference so a status identifies the correct assertion when multiple manifests are reported together. He will submit a PR.

The group discussed whether `cawg.identity.well-formed` can accompany an untrusted-credential failure. Matthew argued that once certificate trust validation fails, the verifier should report that failure rather than also report the assertion as well-formed. Eric agreed that the statuses conflict; Matthew will propose clarifying text.

*ACTION:* Matthew to propose a clarification for issue #290 on whether `cawg.identity.well-formed` can accompany an untrusted-credential failure.

The discussion also covered how identity-assertion failure codes relate to C2PA status reporting. Eric noted that CAWG 1.3 uses its own X.509 failure codes rather than reusing C2PA status codes. Matthew will draft the related clarification.

*ACTION:* Matthew to draft a clarification for issue #289 on identity-assertion failure codes and C2PA manifest validity.

🎥 _46'27":_ Makki presented a proposal for a delegated-creator assertion describing who performs creative work on behalf of an organization. Potential delegate types include an employee, an AI system, or an independent creator such as a contractor; the organization would remain responsible. The group agreed to form a task force to develop a proposal, with Makki as chair. Eric encouraged participation from news organizations, including the BBC and IPTC.

🎥 _50'40":_ Makki also introduced a proposed assertion to describe AI's role in a creative workflow, such as whether work was made without AI assistance, co-created with AI, or commissioned. Members said this is a contested topic that warrants a focused discussion and regulatory review across relevant jurisdictions. The group agreed to combine this work with the delegated-creator task force; Makki will organize it.

*ACTION:* Makki to invite members to a task force on delegated creators and AI's role in creative work, and bring proposals back to the working group.
